# Clearvale template RSA: data deviations and how they are handled

Source: audit of all 90 sessions' PsychoPy logs (`scripts/audit_stim.py`) and Flywheel DICOM metadata
(`notebooks/04_run_lengths_from_flywheel.ipynb`). Event files are generated by `scripts/make_events.py`.

**Run length changed mid-study.** WM runs were 710 volumes (326.6 s) for the 32 sessions scanned through
2024-01-29 and 770 volumes (354.2 s) from 2024-02-01 (56 sessions); localizer runs were 1240 volumes (570.4 s)
throughout (TR = 0.46 s). Each run's events end at its actual scan length, taken from Flywheel's DICOM volume
count. Three aborted runs (sub-036, labelled `_ignore-BIDS`) are excluded; sub-036 runs 04–08 were acquired on a
second visit (2024-03-07).

**Final WM trial logged without labels.** In 193 WM runs (27 sessions) PsychoPy recorded the timing of trial 36
but not its stimulus or operation. Because the trial order is fixed per trial list and run (verified identical
across all sessions), these labels were restored from sessions that logged them and flagged
`label_source = recovered` in the events files.

**Response windows extending past the end of the scan.** Windows are truncated at the last volume; an event is
dropped only when no volume of its window was acquired.
- *WM timecourse (0–13.8 s, 30 TRs):* in 710-volume runs, the last trial of each run (253 events, 32 subjects)
  keeps 13–20 of 30 TRs; in 770-volume runs, 228 events keep 27–29 TRs. Late time bins therefore average over
  slightly fewer trials, mostly in the early cohort.
- *WM encoding fidelity (onset + 4.6 s window, 6 TRs):* 126 events in 710-volume runs are averaged over 3–5 of
  6 volumes.
- *Localizer templates:* in sub-017 D1 run-01 and sub-026 A1 run-01 the stimulus computer fell 12–16 s behind
  schedule (about twice the usual per-trial lag plus one stall), so the last images of the run were shown after
  the scan ended or too late for their response window. Five presentations (sub-017: 2; sub-026: 3; all negative
  faces) have no data and are dropped, leaving those exemplars' templates built from 2 rather than 3
  presentations. They contribute nothing to the localizer GLMs.

**Early stimulus list (19 sessions, Dec 2023 – Jan 2024, sub-001–029).** The WM switch cue used 5 words with no
localizer image (blackberry, blueberry, paint roller, shears, toolbox; 373 switch trials). These trials receive
no `switch_item` score but are scored for target, related, other, and `switch_category`. The same list's
localizer showed 12 negative faces 2 or 4 times instead of 3; templates average whatever presentations exist.

**File-name differences between tasks.** Two images are named differently in the localizer and WM lists
(`Animal.Carcass.1.jpg` / `Animal.carcass.1.jpg`; `yellowstone_1.png` / `yellowstone_1.jpg`). Stimuli are matched
on a normalized identifier (lowercase, trimmed, extension removed), so every WM image has a localizer template.

**Sessions not analysed as standard.** sub-104 S1 is a pilot (no localizer, a `replace` operation, no condition
codes) and cannot be analysed with this method. sub-023 B1 has 5 of 8 WM runs.

**Missing rating responses.** 2,228 localizer trials have no rating, almost all from sub-003 and sub-005
(260 and 270, apparently not recorded). Ratings are not used in the RSA; the images were presented as scheduled.

**Decided.** Truncated response windows are kept and averaged over the volumes acquired, rather than dropping
events whose window is incomplete.

**Open decisions.** (1) Whether to analyse the 710- and 770-volume cohorts together or include cohort as a
covariate. (2) Subject inclusion list -- revisit later: sub-104 (pilot) and sub-023 (5 of 8 WM runs). For
now subject-level outputs are generated for every session that the method can run on.
